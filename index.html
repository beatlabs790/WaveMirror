Everything you need to know about WaveMirror

Is WaveMirror completely free to watch? +
Yes! WaveMirror is 100% free with no paid subscriptions, credit cards, or accounts required.
How do I switch servers or episodes? +
In the player modal, click Server 1 (vidsrc.pm), Server 2 (vidsrc.xyz), or Server 3 (vidsrc.to). For TV series, use the Season & Episode dropdowns to switch episodes instantly!
Why doesn't WaveMirror open new browser tabs? +
We redesigned the entire playback experience to run inside custom in-app video modals and tabbed server players, ensuring seamless, clutter-free streaming.
